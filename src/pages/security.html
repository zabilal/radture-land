Security & compliance

Private and secure by design.

Radture is built with privacy and security by design, aligned with global healthcare and data protection standards, so imaging can move between facilities without putting patient data at risk.

Standards

Standards we align with.

Radture’s approach to privacy, interoperability and clinical data follows these regulations and standards.

GDPREU

General Data Protection Regulation

The EU framework for how personal data is collected, processed and protected.

HIPAAUS

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act

US rules for safeguarding the privacy and security of protected health information.

NDPRNG

Nigeria Data Protection Regulation

Nigeria’s regulation for the lawful processing and protection of personal data.

HL7Global

Health Level Seven

Standards for exchanging clinical and administrative data between healthcare systems.

FHIRGlobal

Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources

HL7’s API-based standard for sharing electronic health records.

ICD-11WHO

International Classification of Diseases, 11th revision

The World Health Organization’s global standard for recording diagnoses.

Safeguards

Protection at every handoff.

Every stage of the imaging workflow, from acquisition to delivery, is designed to keep studies and reports in the right hands.

Secure connectivity

PACS integration uses DICOM, DICOMweb and VPN-based connectivity, so studies move between facilities over secured connections.

Controlled access

Access permissions, secure login and activity monitoring keep imaging records available only to the people who need them.

Traceable workflows

Case tracking and a centralized admin dashboard give teams shared visibility and traceability across every handoff.

Secure delivery

Reports reach referring clinicians and patients through RadtureLink instead of CDs, printouts or unsecured email.
