Capabilities

Everything between the scan and the signed report.

Radture Core structures how studies move, connects the systems you already run, and gives your team oversight of every case.

Structured, reliable case flow

  • Automated assignment of imaging studies to radiologists based on defined rules
  • Built-in approval workflows for double-reading and final clinical sign-off
  • Follow-up scheduling automation to ensure no study is left incomplete

Connected clinical systems

  • Direct EHR synchronization to link patient demographics and scan data
  • Secure PACS integration using DICOM, DICOMweb and VPN-based connectivity
  • Seamless information flow powered by Radture’s internal and external APIs

Reporting, delivery & oversight

  • Smart reporting console with customizable templates
  • Secure report delivery to referring clinicians and patients via RadtureLink
  • Centralized admin dashboard for case tracking, turnaround times and reading analytics
