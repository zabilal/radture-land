Our view

We see radiology differently.

Radture reimagines radiology to make it faster, smarter and more connected. RadtureLink gives patients and healthcare providers instant, secure access to radiology reports, enhancing collaboration and streamlining follow-up.

As soon as a report is finalized, patients can view, download and share their scans with referring physicians or specialists. A built-in mobile DICOM viewer lets patients and physicians review scans anytime, anywhere.
