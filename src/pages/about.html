About Radture

Shaping the future of medical imaging, one report at a time.

Radture is built by Ultradeck Labs to connect hospitals, imaging centers and radiologists, so every scan moves from acquisition to a signed report without delay.

Who we are

Built for hospitals and imaging centers.

Radture brings workflow automation and seamless PACS integration to radiology. Built for hospitals and imaging centers, it improves efficiency, speeds up reporting and keeps radiology reports accurate and structured.

The platform supports radiologists with AI-assisted reporting, smart worklist management and automated follow-up tracking, enabling faster diagnoses and better patient outcomes. With Radture, healthcare providers can streamline operations, enhance collaboration and deliver high-quality care.
